What you're seeing

Each connector on the harness carries an always-visible label with its actual connector ID (B104, B204, B307, and so on). Orbit the model and the labels track their connectors and tuck away when they rotate behind the harness.

This answers your notes

  • Labels show the real connector ID text, not 1, 2, 3.
  • Always visible, no clicking required.
  • One global style (font, weight, justification) set once in CSS, not per hotspot.
  • Tight boxes sized to the text.
This is the embeddable viewer component, not the no-code hotspot editor you tried. In the editor those settings are fixed; embedded like this, the label is ordinary HTML/CSS, so you get the fast 2D-callout read, plus orbit, zoom, and isolate on top.
