  • Detail: Full = seen in the hero view, model and texture it properly. Normal = visible, keep it clean. Simple = a shape and a color.
  • Clean quads where it matters (bezel, shell, speaker bar). Triangulated areas are fine where they will not show. No ngons on curved surfaces.
  • Apply all transforms. Scale 1, rotation 0 on every object before export. No empties, no hidden objects, no modifiers left unapplied.
  • If the renders show something not on this list, model it and tell us. Do not invent what is not in the renders.

5What you deliver

  • GLB: E300AG32PBR_v1.glb. Meters, real-world size, glTF Y-up (Blender exporter default), no Draco or KTX compression, textures embedded, all objects and materials named as above.
  • Blender file: E300AG32PBR_v1.blend with all textures packed (File > External Data > Pack Resources). Blender 4.2 or newer.
  • Origin: bottom center, on the floor between the feet. Front of the TV faces -Y in Blender (the default front view).
  • Budget: under 100,000 triangles for the whole model. Expect 40,000 to 80,000.
  • Textures: PNG, 2048 px per material (grille up to 4096). Base color, metal/roughness, normal; opacity only where used.
  • Check renders: three quick renders from Blender (front, back, three-quarter) under any neutral HDRI, so we can compare against the Element renders before upload.
  • Notes: a few lines on what you built, anything you could not read from the renders, and time spent.
  • Delivery by Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer link. VNTANA uploads to the Element account; you do not need platform access for this job.

6Pre-delivery QA

  1. Bounding box is 724 × 455 × 140 mm (within 5 mm). Screen about 697 mm wide inside the bezel.
  2. Origin at bottom center on the floor; front faces -Y; all transforms applied; no empties or hidden objects.
  3. Every object and material named with the E300AG32PBR_ / MAT_ names above. Nothing called Cube, Plane or Material.001.
  4. Port panel: 10 connectors in the order listed in section 1; labels legible at 2048 px.
  5. Grille holes read correctly from 1 m and from 20 cm (texture resolution).
  6. Under 100k triangles; no ngons on curved surfaces; no flipped normals (check with face orientation).
  7. GLB has no Draco, all textures embedded, and looks the same in a glTF viewer (for example gltf-viewer.donmccurdy.com) as in Blender.
  8. Side-by-side with Front_Straight-On, Back and Side_Left: proportions, bezel width, speaker bar height and stance angle match.
One round of revisions is included. Deliver when you have gone through this list; we review in the VNTANA viewer and send one consolidated fix list.
