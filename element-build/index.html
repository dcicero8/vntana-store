0What we need

Create a 3D model, in one colorway (black), of the Element 32" Portable TV using the provided reference images and specs. The model must be true to size, because it will be viewed in 3D and in AR. A CAD file is not available as a starting point.

  • Deliverables: a GLB plus your source files (section 5). We review the GLB in the VNTANA viewer.
  • Effort: about 2 to 3 working days. Most of the variance is in the ports: use a port library or free online connector models if you have them; modeling them from scratch is fine but slower. Agree the delivery date with us before you start.
  • Product page:UPDATED NOV 3 none yet; the TV is pre-launch, so the renders, the bottom photo and the logo art below are the complete reference set. Element has confirmed the open questions (Nov 3), so nothing on this page is waiting on the customer.
  • Tools: any 3D package. The GLB is what matters.

  • The Detail column says how much work each part deserves. Full: a part people will see up close in the viewer (screen, bezel, grille, module), so model its shape accurately and texture it fully. Normal: clearly visible but secondary (connectors, logos), model it cleanly at a lower polygon count. Simple: barely seen (feet, LED, label), a basic shape with a flat material is enough.
  • Mesh quality: clean quad topology on the large visible surfaces while you work; the GLB export will triangulate everything, which is expected. No ngons on curved surfaces, no flipped normals, no overlapping or doubled faces.
  • Apply all transforms. Scale 1, rotation 0 on every object before export. No empties, no hidden objects, no modifiers left unapplied.
  • If the renders show something not on this list, model it and tell us. Do not invent what is not in the renders.

4Materials

Materials must use the PBR metallic/roughness workflow with 2K (2048 × 2048 px) textures; the grille may use 4K. Use a unique material for each part as listed in the table below, named exactly as shown. Each material needs its own UV layout, with the UV islands filling the full 0 to 1 UV space to make the most of the texture resolution. Scale the UVs so that logos, labels and other fine details get enough texel density to stay sharp and legible in the 3D viewer. Procedural shading is fine to author with, but everything must be baked to textures: the GLB is what the customer sees.

5What you deliver

  • GLB: E300AG32PBR_v1.glb. Meters, real-world size, standard glTF Y-up, no Draco or KTX compression, textures embedded, all objects and materials named as above.
  • Source scene: your working file with textures packed or collected (.blend, .max, .ma or equivalent), so we can make small fixes later.
  • Texturing source: if you texture in Substance Painter or similar, the project file (.spp) as well, plus the exported texture set.
  • Origin and orientation: origin at bottom center, on the floor between the feet. The TV stands upright and the screen faces the viewer in the default front view (glTF +Z, which is Blender -Y).
  • Budget: under 100,000 triangles for the whole model. Expect 40,000 to 80,000.
  • Textures: PNG, 2048 px per material (grille up to 4096). Base color, metallic/roughness, normal; opacity only where used.
  • Notes: a few lines on what you built, anything you could not read from the renders, and time spent. No renders needed: we check the GLB in the VNTANA viewer. Viewport screenshots are welcome if they help explain something.
  • Delivery by Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer link. VNTANA uploads to the customer's account; you do not need platform access for this job.

6Pre-delivery QA

  1. Bounding box is 724 × 455 × 140 mm (within 5 mm). Screen about 697 mm wide inside the bezel.
  2. Origin at bottom center on the floor; the screen faces the viewer in the default front view (glTF +Z); all transforms applied; no empties or hidden objects.
  3. Every object and material named with the E300AG32PBR_ / MAT_ names above. Nothing called Cube, Plane or Material.001.
  4. Port panel: 10 connectors in the order listed in section 1; labels legible at 2048 px.
  5. Grille holes read correctly from 1 m and from 20 cm (texture resolution).
  6. Under 100k triangles; no ngons on curved surfaces; no flipped normals (check with face orientation).
  7. GLB has no Draco, all textures embedded, and looks the same in a glTF viewer (for example gltf-viewer.donmccurdy.com) as in your 3D package.
  8. Side-by-side with Front_Straight-On, Back and Side_Left: proportions, bezel width, speaker bar height and stance angle match.
  9. NEW NOV 3Underside matches Bottom: five pads in the right places, slotted plate under the module, vent rows; the logos use Element's vector art.
