The wilbert-configurator 1.1.0 build needs these 8 color texture files served here.
They are NOT shipped in the tgz (build/ only). Get them from Neven and drop them in
this folder (assets/wilbert/configurator/textures/). Filenames are fixed by the build:

  black-body-base.jpg
  black-body-cover.jpg
  navy-body-base.jpg
  navy-body-cover.jpg
  silver-accent-base.png
  silver-accent-cover.png
  pink-accent-base.png
  pink-accent-cover.png

Until they are present:
  - base-color="white"  works (native GLB state, loads no texture)
  - navy / black / silver / pink  degrade to the baked look (console.warn, no throw)

The page sets texture-root="assets/wilbert/configurator/textures" on <wilbert-configurator>.
