Confirmed on the Sept 16 call

Answers captured on the Sept 16 PoC sync with Chris Sides and Jerry Pavlik (Wilbert). Same rules as the White Venetian, with the Triune specifics below.

Models · geometry

  • Triune body is one mesh. Stainless, bronze, and copper are the same geometry with the material swapped, not three meshes.
  • Cameo Rose and Veteran are pre-configured Triunes. Specific set-color models (Cameo Rose is pink with a white face), not free variants. Build each as its fixed configuration.
  • Lid is one solid piece. Molded as a single unit, so model it as one mesh even though the DWG breaks it into pieces.
  • Corner notch is real geometry, keep it. It is the cable lift point, not a normal map.
  • Show two states. A hero with the cover off and set to the side, and a closed version shown in place.

Hardware

  • Three handle types across the line. Two are thin lifting hoops for pulling the lid off; the third is a thicker, more elegant handle. Some models have none.
  • Nameplate is 3D and optional. Stands off the surface, toggles on and off like the emblems.
  • Foot label is 2D. A printed metal plate adhered flat, wording varies by vault (Wilbert Triune).

Emblems · personalization

  • Emblems are 3D: 13 designs plus Veteran (14). In bronze, copper, and stainless. The Veteran Triune needs its own Veteran emblem, which is not one of the standard picks.
  • Everything else on the top is 2D. Cover prints and appliqués are maps, easy to add without modeling.

Interior · scope

  • Liner color is independent of the body. A white marble insert pairs with many exterior colorways while keeping a white marbled interior.
  • Infant caskets and vaults are out of scope.
  • Measurements are complete. We have the DWG files and the dimension spreadsheet.
