4-Bolt Flange Bearings

for conveyors

Square 4-bolt flange-mount bearing units for conveyor shafts, built for high radial loads and easy field replacement. Pre-lubricated inserts seat into a cast or stamped housing and bolt flush to the conveyor frame.

  • Mounting4-bolt square flange
  • Bore range1/2" to 2-7/16"
  • HousingCast iron · stainless · thermoplastic
  • LockingSet screw · eccentric collar
