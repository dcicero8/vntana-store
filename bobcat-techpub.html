    Your Questions: Quick Answers

    1Can hotspot text boxes be sized for small labels?Yes

    In the no-code platform embed, hotspot styling is fixed, which is why Mike couldn't find a setting. When you embed with our web component (as on this page), each hotspot's label is your own HTML element, so you control size, font, and width entirely with CSS. Small labels like the samples above are straightforward.

    2Can hotspot text be always visible?Yes

    By default a label opens on click, but since you own the label markup in the web component, you can render it always-on. The numbered callouts and labels above are all always-visible.

    3Is there a GLB file-size limit?Skip the GLB export

    There's a practical upload ceiling, and KeyShot GLB exports are usually the culprit: KeyShot is known for exporting bloated, not-very-clean GLBs (uncompressed, very large textures, no mesh compression), so even a simple model can balloon to hundreds of MB. The easiest fix is to not export the GLB from KeyShot at all. Send us the KeyShot file and we convert and optimize it into a lean, web-ready GLB. That is exactly what our pipeline is built to do.

    4How do we retain KeyShot decals?Send the KeyShot file

    Same approach helps here, and we should align on what "decals" means for you: surface graphics (printed logos / labels baked onto the part), or interactive callout bubbles you want to appear in the viewer. For surface graphics, sending us the KeyShot file lets us carry them through without you baking anything. For interactive callouts, those are built as hotspots in the viewer (see the labels above). Happy to confirm which one Mike has in mind.
