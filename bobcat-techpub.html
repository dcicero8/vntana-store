Your Questions: Quick Answers

1Can hotspot text boxes be sized for small labels?Yes

In the no-code platform embed, hotspot styling is fixed, which is why Mike couldn't find a setting. When you embed with our web component, each hotspot's label is your own HTML element, so you control size, font, and width entirely with CSS. Small labels like the samples above are straightforward.

2Can hotspot text be always visible?Yes

By default a label opens on click, but since you own the label markup in the web component, you can render it always-on. The numbered callouts and labels above are all always-visible.

3Is there a GLB file-size limit?Skip the GLB export

There's a practical upload ceiling, and KeyShot GLB exports are usually the culprit: they come out uncompressed, with very large textures and no mesh compression, so even a simple model can balloon to hundreds of MB. The easiest fix is to not export the GLB from KeyShot at all. Send us the KeyShot file and we convert and optimize it into a lean, web-ready GLB for you. That is exactly what our pipeline is built to do.

4How do we retain KeyShot decal labels?Send the KeyShot file

Same approach solves this. When you export a GLB from KeyShot, decals do not carry over (they are not a glTF concept). If you send us the KeyShot file instead, we convert it with the decals in place, so you do not have to bake or flatten anything yourself.
