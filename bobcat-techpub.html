What it is

The Hydraulic X-Change coupler is the connection point that routes the loader's auxiliary hydraulic flow out to a powered attachment. It lets an operator hook up a hydraulic attachment (auger, grapple, breaker, and the like) without hand-tools, and carries both the pressure and return lines through one tidy assembly.

How it is set up & rigged

  1. The coupler body mounts at the front of the loader, tied into the machine's auxiliary hydraulic circuit.
  2. The two flat-face quick-connect ports mate with the matching couplers on the attachment, making the hydraulic connection.
  3. The hoses route the pressure line and the return line between the machine and the attachment.
  4. To connect or disconnect, hydraulic pressure is relieved first, the flat-face couplers are pushed together, and the connection is locked. Flat-face couplers keep the ports clean and spill-free.
Why 3D: a technician can orbit the assembly, see exactly where each line runs, and identify the right part before ordering or servicing, instead of reading it off a flat exploded drawing.
