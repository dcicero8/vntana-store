Series 2000B-FP Double Check Valve Assembly

Cast bronze double check assembly with UL-listed, gear-operated slow-close shutoffs and pre-wired tamper switches. Built for non-health-hazard cross-connections on fire protection lines, with every internal part serviced through a single top cover.
