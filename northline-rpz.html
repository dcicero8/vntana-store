Northline Flow Controls

NRP-200 Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly

Lead-free bronze RPZ for high-hazard cross-connections. Two independent checks with a differential relief valve between them, quarter-turn ball valve shutoffs, and a bolt-on air-gap drain. Each check has its own top cover, so you can service one without touching the other.
