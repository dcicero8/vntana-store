Prerequisites & safety

Review this manual fully before performing any work on the pump or motor.

Handling: do not hoist the pump by the eyebolts — they are for removing the back pull-out assembly only. Hoist an assembled pump with a sling under the suction flange and under the rear of the bearing frame.

Before disassembly: make sure all replacement parts are on hand.
