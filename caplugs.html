RPO Series

Threaded Plugs for SAE Straight Thread Ports

A molded O-ring seal gives these plugs dual sealing on SAE straight-thread ports, while the flange lip blocks contaminants. The slotted 12-point head installs and removes with a socket, wrench or by hand.
