3Rules of Delivery

Every asset, every family. If a rule cannot be met on a given part, flag it in your QA notes rather than guessing. This section is the acceptance standard: assets that miss these are returned.

New Latest from Taco (Oct 2): the image angle reference guide is confirmed, see Render / template setup below. The 007e-family faceplate is being redesigned across multiple departments (target end of October), so faceplate artwork is on hold. Engineering is checking whether SolidWorks source files are available (update expected Thursday). Contacts: Ken owns materials / art direction; Jess & Jai own angles / render outputs.

What you deliver (per asset)

  • GLB (glTF binary, PBR metal/roughness, textures embedded). This is the primary deliverable. New Export it from the delivered .blend: same meshes, same names, same materials. A GLB merged into one mesh or one material is returned.
  • Packed Blender file (.blend) with all textures packed in.
  • Parts manifest (CSV): one row per serviceable part, with the real part name.
  • Substance source files New, required: the Substance Painter project (.spp) per asset, with layers intact. Library materials as .sbs + .sbsar.
  • Self-check renders New: the hero shot plus one 4K close-up of the cast metal, as PNGs.
  • 4K texture maps folder: no longer needed; the maps are packed in the .blend.

Geometry

  • One mesh per serviceable part, placed at the scene root. No empty wrappers, no nested parent hierarchy around parts.
  • No mega-part. Do not merge the whole product into a single mesh.
  • Keep all internal parts. Do not delete internals just because they are hidden in the assembled view.
  • High-poly is the primary deliverable and must stay smooth at 4K. Do not pre-decimate.
  • Nested shells (a part inside a housing) need at least 2mm clearance so they do not poke through after optimization.
  • Flag artifacts, do not reproduce them. If the source CAD has a defect, note it; do not model the defect faithfully.

Naming

  • Real part names on every mesh. Use the replacement-parts naming where available.
  • For the first run, group the obvious hardware (screws, bolts, washers) into a single named group per fastener type. We will revisit finer renaming later. Build it logically now.
Note on part names: STEP AP203 export strips component names, so circulators arrive as unnamed solids. Where you cannot get a real name from CAD, name from the catalog / replacement-parts sheet and flag it.

Origin, scale, orientation

  • Upright and grounded. Origin at bottom-center of the product.
  • Real-world scale in meters.
  • No animations, curves, constraints, or empties in the delivered file.
  • New No scale on any object. Apply all transforms (scale 1.0 everywhere). Never scale the product to fit a camera; move the camera instead.

Materials & UVs

  • glTF PBR metal/roughness, textures embedded in the GLB.
  • One material per physical material. Do not split one physical material across several, or merge two real materials into one. New In the .blend this means separate Blender materials taken from the signed-off library, named as in the library, not regions of one baked atlas. Cast metals must show real sand-cast grain at true scale.
  • Single 0 to 1 UV space per material, with seams hidden on non-visible faces.
  • Faceplates and labels are textures, not modeled geometry. Confirm the version/label artwork before baking it in.
  • Side-of-casing label. Decided Oct 9 For 0034e and 0026e the side marking is a sticker label: deliver the Flat Label version only, using the side label layout (.ai), never the old CAD text. Put the label text in its own material on the label part (Taco may ask for lighter text). Keep the casing shape as the CAD has it. Other families: ask before building the label; some newer products are etched.
New Faceplate on hold. Taco is redesigning the 007e-family faceplate across multiple departments (target end of October). Do not bake faceplate artwork yet; model the surface so a texture can drop in later. Ken owns materials / art direction; Jess & Jai own angles / render outputs.
