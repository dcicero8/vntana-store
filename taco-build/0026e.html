3Upload, QA & Approval

When the family is built, self-QA against this checklist first, then upload to the VNTANA platform as a Draft and notify VNTANA for review. Do not mark anything final. VNTANA does the final approval.

Pre-delivery QA (check every item before uploading)

  • All 4 deliverables present per variant: GLB, packed .blend, parts manifest CSV, 4K maps.
  • One mesh per serviceable part at scene root. No mega-part, no empty wrappers.
  • All internal parts kept. Nested shells have 2mm clearance.
  • Every mesh has a real part name (or a flagged catalog name). Fasteners grouped logically.
  • Upright, grounded, origin bottom-center, real-world meters.
  • glTF PBR metal/roughness, textures embedded, one material per physical material.
  • Single 0 to 1 UV per material, seams hidden.
  • Faceplate / labels / screens are textures, artwork version confirmed.
  • 6 ortho cameras + detail cams present. Neutral HDRI packed, no extra lights, AgX.
  • Renders smooth at 4K. Source artifacts flagged in notes, not reproduced.
  • All variants share the same body geometry; only the noted differences change.

Upload to VNTANA platform

  • Upload with the Preserve Meshes and UVs pipeline (do not let the platform re-mesh).
  • Use the Taco project preset: Pivot None, Scaling None, obstructed-geometry removal OFF.
  • Save as Draft. Do not publish.
  • Fill the parts manifest so the platform part list matches your named meshes.

Approval flow

  • Step 1: 3D Team self-QA: pass the checklist above.
  • Step 2: Upload as Draft and notify VNTANA that 0026e is ready for review.
  • Step 3: VNTANA review: we check geometry, naming, scale, materials, and the render set. Feedback comes back on this family thread.
  • Step 4: Revise or approve. Once VNTANA approves, VNTANA sets it Live. The 3D Team never publishes.
