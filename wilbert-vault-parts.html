Vault Cover (Lid)
Cover (3D)
Sloped cover that seals to the base. One mesh, shared across the Basic line.

Polished Marble Top
Cover (3D) · material zone
Polished-marble material on the top face of the cover; stucco on the sides.
3
Cover Print
Decal (2D)
Legacy Custom Series™ / Legacy Series™ print. Plane-mesh overlay conformed to the cover; not geometry.
4
Nameplate
Decal (3D & 2D)
Low-poly plate + 2D text decal. Standard or U.S. flag (Veteran) variant.
5
Vault Base (Body)
Base (3D)
Lower concrete receptacle that houses the casket. One mesh, bottom-center origin.
6
Interior Liner
Interior (3D) · internal
Reinforcing liner fitted inside base + cover (not visible in this view). Plain or marble-look.
7
Emblem
Hardware (3D)
Metal emblem on the carapace (bronze, copper, or stainless). True geometry; 13 designs, the wreath being the densest. Not always present on the White Venetian.
8
Foot Label
Decal (2D)
Small identification label at the foot of the lid. 2D overlay. Confirm placement with Wilbert.

To confirm with Wilbert

What we need pointed out and validated so the 3D scope is right. Built from the website; please correct anything wrong.

Models · geometry

  • The Wilbert Bronze — confirm it is a distinct mesh (looks like a different cast).
  • Triune (bronze / copper) — confirm this is one model in two color variants, not two meshes.
  • Standard Triunes (Stainless, Cameo Rose, Veteran) — confirm these are color variants of one mesh.
  • Corner "notch" on the Basic / Standard line — real geometry, or a surface relief we can do with a normal map?
  • Is the cover always shown off and leaning against the base?

Hardware

  • Handles — how many distinct handles? (we see ~2–3: Continental, Wilbert Bronze, small ones on Monticello / Salute). Which models have them, and how many variants?
  • Nameplate — standard on every model? Placement and variants?
  • Foot label (plate at the foot of the lid) — placement, and is it always present?

Emblems · personalization

  • Emblems — the site shows 13; are there more ("ask about other available emblems")? Confirm bronze / copper / stainless finishes (13 x 3 = 39).
  • Salute emblem — exclusive to the Salute model, or available on others?
  • Legacy Custom tiers (Silver / Gold / Platinum) — is that the metal the print is applied to?
  • Which components can be turned on / off (e.g. the Delane pinstripe)?

Interior · scope

  • Interior liner — color variants, or separate meshes? Is the marble-look liner available per model?
  • Confirm the exterior colors available for each model.
  • Infant caskets / vaults — in scope?
  • Measurements — our mock used dimensions off the website (SKU D1007); confirm the real dimensions.
