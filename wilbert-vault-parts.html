Components

Vault Cover (Lid)
Cover (3D)
Sloped cover that seals to the base. One mesh, shared across the Basic line.
2
Polished Marble Top
Cover (3D) · material zone
Polished-marble material on the top face of the cover; stucco on the sides.
3
Cover Print
Decal (2D)
Legacy Custom Series™ / Legacy Series™ print. Plane-mesh overlay conformed to the cover; not geometry.
4
Nameplate
Decal (3D & 2D)
Low-poly plate + 2D text decal. Standard or U.S. flag (Veteran) variant.
5
Vault Base (Body)
Base (3D)
Lower concrete receptacle that houses the casket. One mesh, bottom-center origin.
6
Interior Liner
Interior (3D) · internal
Reinforcing liner fitted inside base + cover (not visible in this view). Plain or marble-look.
7
Emblem
Hardware (3D)
Metal emblem on the carapace (bronze, copper, or stainless). True geometry; 13 designs, the wreath being the densest. Not always present on the White Venetian.
8
Foot Label
Decal (2D)
Small identification label at the foot of the lid. 2D overlay. Confirm placement with Wilbert.
