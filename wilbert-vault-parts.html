Components

1
Vault Cover (Lid)
Cover (3D)
Sloped cover that seals to the base. Molded as one solid piece, so model it as a single mesh even though the DWG shows multiple pieces.
2
Polished Marble Top
Cover material zone (2D print)
Printed vinyl covering that mimics marble, thermoformed and adhered to the lid. No real dimension, so we do it as a 2D material map on the top face, not separate geometry. With no customization the top is just polished marble.
3
Cover Print
Decal (2D)
Legacy Custom Series™ / Legacy Series™ print. Plane-mesh overlay conformed to the cover; not geometry.
4
Nameplate
Hardware (3D) · optional
A plate that stands off the surface, so true 3D, with a 2D text decal. Optional on every model, toggles on and off like the emblems. Wilbert nameplate art owed.
5
Vault Base (Body)
Base (3D)
Lower concrete receptacle that houses the casket. One mesh, bottom-center origin.
6
Interior Liner (not pictured here)
Interior (3D) · internal
Reinforcing liner fitted inside base + cover (not visible in this view). Color is independent of the body: the white marble insert lets one body pair with many exterior colorways while keeping a white marbled interior. Standard Venetian ships in its cream colorway.
7
Emblem (not pictured here)
Hardware (3D)
Metal emblem on the carapace (bronze, copper, or stainless). True geometry; 13 designs plus a Veteran emblem (14 total), the wreath being the densest. Optional, not always present on the White Venetian. Veteran emblem art owed.
8
Foot Label
Decal (2D)
Metal plate at the foot of the lid, printed and adhered flat with no raised element, so 2D. A slightly recessed logo whose wording varies by vault (Wilbert Venetian, Wilbert Triune). Foot-label art owed.

Confirmed on the Sept 16 call

Answers captured on the Sept 16 PoC sync with Chris Sides and Jerry Pavlik (Wilbert). This is the build spec for the White Venetian.

Models · geometry

  • Lid is one solid piece. Molded as a single unit, so model it as one mesh even though the DWG breaks it into pieces.
  • Marble top is a printed vinyl. Thermoformed plastic that mimics marble, adhered to the lid with no real dimension. Do it as a 2D material map, not geometry.
  • Side texture stays a 2D map. The two-tone texture is molded into the concrete on the real product, but we represent it with a map to keep the mesh light, not modeled relief.
  • Corner notch is real geometry, keep it. It is the cable lift point. Model it in, do not fake it with a normal map.
  • Show two states. A hero with the cover off and set to the side, and a closed version shown in place.

Hardware

  • Three handle types. Two are thin lifting hoops for pulling the lid off (e.g. Monticello); the third is a thicker, more elegant handle. Some models have no handles at all.
  • Nameplate is 3D and optional. A plate that stands off the surface, toggles on and off on every model like the emblems.
  • Foot label is 2D. A printed metal plate adhered flat to the lid, no raised element. Wording varies by vault (Wilbert Venetian, Wilbert Triune).

Emblems · personalization

  • Emblems are 3D: 13 designs plus Veteran (14). Modeled as true geometry, in bronze, copper, and stainless finishes.
  • Everything else on the top is 2D. Cover prints, appliqués, applications, and all top graphics are maps, not geometry.
  • Cover prints stay 2D and are easy to add. New print options do not need modeling; the emblems are the pieces we must dial in.

Interior · scope

  • Liner color is independent of the body. The white marble insert lets one body pair with many exterior colorways (blue and silver, red and silver) while keeping a white marbled interior. Standard Venetian ships cream.
  • Infant caskets and vaults are out of scope.
  • Measurements are complete. We have the DWG files and the dimension spreadsheet.
