3D Asset Intake Playbook
VNTANA internal: names clients and open bugs

What we learned cleaning up the Costco Mexico pilot (42 vendor assets, two days, Oct 21-22) plus the Accenture and J&J batches. End state: 26 of 42 verified ready for production. Everything below is a pattern we hit more than once, written up as observations and ideas for Jure + ModelOps to review: what we saw, what we think caused it, and what might help.

1What vendors actually send (the 16 failure types)

Counts are from the Costco batch alone. Median asset had 2-3 of these at once; only ONE of the 42 arrived with nothing to fix.

  • Floated in AR (24 assets). Origin not at the base. One was LIVE and hovering 76 cm. Fix: Pivot Bottom Center. Idea: default pivot to bottom-center, or warn when a model's base is far from y=0.
  • Pre-compressed uploads (11). Draco'd files, including our own outputs re-uploaded as source. Optimization rejects them; Convert Only passes them through unfixed. Fix: decompress, reprocess. Idea: detect and decompress at ingest.
  • Required-extension exports (3 LG TVs). extensionsRequired with Draco/texture_transform means the converter must reject the file by spec, and it failed with no explanation. Fix: bake transforms into UVs. Idea: read extensionsRequired at upload and say so in the error.
  • Incomplete uploads (7). .gltf manifests without their _resources folder; naked USD files without textures (a black FoodSaver arrived white). The geometry never left the vendor's machine. Idea: reject a .gltf whose buffer URIs are unresolvable, with a message naming the missing files.
  • Colorless materials. Materials named NEGRO (black) exported with NO baseColorFactor: glTF defaults that to white. Related: omitted metallicFactor defaults to 1.0 (gray-metal wood). Validator-friendly checks.
  • Render junk inside. lux_root, Helper001, Particle_View, leftover cameras: inflates bounds, breaks AR placement. Idea: strip empties/cameras/lights at ingest, or at least exclude them from bounds.
  • Degenerate slivers. 1-2 triangle, zero-width, meter-long CAD centerlines. Made a 2 m satellite read 13.9 m and orbit around air. Cheap ingest check: near-zero width + large extent = drop it.
  • Transmission glass. Renders opaque in iOS Quick Look. Fix: alpha-blend. Idea: convert on USDZ generation.
  • Also seen: baked leans, wrong units (a 36 m stand mixer), too-heavy Convert Only pass-throughs (901k tris), wrong pipeline (3D Scan on CAD), unreadable baked text, unsupported formats (.max, .3DS), duplicates.

2Two possible platform bugs (repro files available)

Version contamination 2 repro pairs

What we saw: after an asset was re-versioned many times (especially with rotations involved), its output went wrong: a byte-identical GLB then converts straight on a fresh asset and comes out rotated 25 deg with inflated bounds on the churned one. Pairs: cooler 7e910ec2 vs e3e67fe5; sectional 2659200b vs 9f484215 (same file, same day). Workaround we used: fix on a fresh asset instead of in place.

GLB-to-USDZ mismatch likely cause found, workaround in use

Assets with unfrozen node transforms produce USDZ outputs rotated 9-45 deg and floating, while the web GLB is perfect (4 cases). Likely cause: the USDZ path seems to compose node transforms differently. Workaround: OPTIMIZATION.freezeTransforms=true cleared it in every case we tried (now our default). A possible fix: have the converter handle unfrozen transforms, or default freeze on for static assets.

3The QA rig (a possible starting point for ingest checks)

One command (vntana-store/admin/qa-asset.py) runs any GLB through three stages and prints a verdict with the fix per flag. Every check exists because a real asset failed it this month. Jure/ModelOps: this list could be a starting point for ingest validation.

  • Header (seconds): extensionsRequired, empty/rig-only scenes, viz colors, black-metal, omitted factors, junk nodes, zero scales, tiny textures, transmission glass, animation payload, generator provenance.
  • Geometry (Blender): real-world dims vs the PDP, baked-lean solver with false-positive guard, grounding, centering, sliver detection, three renders.
  • AR (USDZ): up axis, metersPerUnit, grounded, dims match the GLB and the real product. In the Costco batch, every asset that passed it also passed Derek's on-device AR check.
  • Bonus integration: Costco's catalog API supplies official names, dimensions and photos per item (pdp-attach.py): names and reference data from the source of truth.

4What we could build (suggestions, roughly by impact)

  • 1. Actionable upload errors. extensionsRequired, unresolvable .gltf buffers, and empty scenes are all detectable in milliseconds at upload. Today they fail without an explanation, so the customer cannot tell a source-file problem from a platform one.
  • 2. Freeze transforms by default (or fix the USDZ composer). Kills the whole phone-AR mismatch class.
  • 3. Look into version contamination. Repro pairs above.
  • 4. Ingest hygiene pass: decompress Draco inputs, strip render junk, drop slivers, warn on output-larger-than-input.
  • 5. Docs: publish the pipeline-by-source intake table + the "before you export" vendor checklist (no pre-compression, embed textures, delete helpers, real PBR values).
