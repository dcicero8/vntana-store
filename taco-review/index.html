1bBronze material review

The bronze example you asked for is on your platform now (swatch below), built from the separator photos Liz sent. This is a material-only test so we agree the look before applying it to the 49-xxx separators.

Bronze swatch, neutral studio lighting.
  • Cast surface — keep. The matte, grainy sand-cast texture matches the real body well.
  • Color — warm it up. The swatch reads dark olive-bronze; the real part is a warmer, lighter yellow-gold brass.
  • Two finish zones. The cast body is matte, but the machined parts (drain plug threads, top air-vent nipple, port faces) are brighter and more polished. A single uniform finish will look wrong; we plan a polished variant for machined surfaces.
Plan (agreed Oct 23): surface is good, color is off. VNTANA is updating the bronze to the lighter "hero" tone and adding a machined finish (the smoother look on machined areas vs the rough cast body). Re-delivering Tue/Wed next week; once Ken signs off, we apply it to all the separators.
