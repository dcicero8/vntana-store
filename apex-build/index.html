Apex satellite platforms: what to build

Apex builds satellite buses. We are building a 3D model of each of their three platforms. Start with Nova. This page has the process and rules for all platforms; each platform page has its specs, options, parts, and QA.

2How the work runs

Confidential: all Apex files (CAD, reference images, this brief) are covered by the NDA. Do not share them outside your team or use them for anything else.
  1. Materials. Build a material cube for each key material and upload it. Apex approves each one in the VNTANA viewer against its reference photo. Approved materials go into the material library (section 5).
  2. Build the model from the VNTANA CAD, with library materials only: Deployed and Stowed first (section 4). Upload the GLBs to the Apex workspace and run the QA check.
  3. Viewer review (VNTANA and Apex). We review your GLBs with Apex in the VNTANA viewer, set the viewer settings (lighting) and pick the render views. We send you the settings and a screenshot of each view.
  4. Render files. Then build the Blender render setup to match those settings and views (section 6) and attach the .blend to each asset. Do not start this before step 3.
  5. Animation. Build the Animated asset last, once Deployed and Stowed are approved.
  6. Review: Apex and VNTANA leave notes as annotations on the asset. Fix, re-upload as a new version.
  • Before you start: send VNTANA a date for each step above, and how many review rounds your quote includes (materials are the step most likely to need more than one).
  • Questions: if something is unclear (which part a material goes on, for example), mark up a screenshot with an arrow and post it in the Slack channel. Use annotation replies only for final decisions or reference requests, and tag Derek (@Derek) so he is notified. Apex sees the annotations.
  • Updates are rolling: upload as you go, so we can review and get Apex feedback early.

Where files live

  • Apex on the VNTANA platform (VNTANA gives you access). Reference photos: Reference assets workspace. Nova CAD: linked on the Nova page. The render template and material library go here too. Upload all your deliveries to the Apex workspace.

4What you deliver (per platform)

  • Three separate assets on the VNTANA platform, each with its own GLB and its own .blend:
Asset nameWhat it shows
<PLATFORM>_DeployedSolar arrays open. The main product asset.
<PLATFORM>_StowedFolded for launch, arrays stowed against the bus.
<PLATFORM>_Animated10-second animation from stowed to deployed. One clip named DEPLOY in the GLB. Rotate the real hinges and SADAs; do not morph or scale parts.
  • Example: NOVA_Deployed, NOVA_Stowed, NOVA_Animated.
  • Render setup: every .blend uses the render setup agreed after the viewer review (section 6). The GLBs come first; the .blend follows.
  • Materials: library materials only. Substance source files (.spp or .sbsar) for every new material.
  • All textures packed in each .blend. One .blend attached per asset.
  • No baked ambient occlusion needed. Renders come from Cycles, which makes real contact shadows, so you do not need full UVs just for an AO bake.
  • Animation: keyframes, so it plays in the GLB. Armature skinning is OK where a part has to bend (the solar panel backing), but test that it exports and plays in the GLB first and tell us the result. No drivers, constraints or simulations in the final file (bake them to keyframes if you use them while working).
  • Textures: 4K (4096 × 4096), so the Cycles renders hold up close.

Starting listCONFIRM with Apex. Match the reference photos in the Apex workspace.

  • Apex website images are accurate for any finish visible in them (Nova page). Use them alongside the photos.
  • No limit on the number of materials. Make as many as it takes to look right, and reuse them wherever the finish is the same (base metals especially).
  • Missing or weak reference? Reply on the Apex annotation with "Additional reference required" and tag Derek. Known gaps: a better silver-coated Teflon photo and a real black MLI / black Kapton photo.

Material cube (for approval)

  • One beveled cube per material, 20 cm, with the texture at real-world scale.
  • Upload each cube to the Apex workspace as an asset named MAT_<NAME>_v1, with its .blend attached and the reference photo it matches. Apex reviews it in the VNTANA viewer (Neutral environment).
  • Not sure between two looks? Put each option on the real part instead (for example one solar panel in blue and one in black) and upload them side by side. Apex picks one. This is faster than going back and forth on cubes.
  • After approval, the material is locked. Use it as is. If a part needs a finish that is not in the library, make a new cube and get it approved first.

6Render template (after the viewer review)

  • When: only after VNTANA and Apex have reviewed your GLBs in the VNTANA viewer (section 2, step 3). We send you the agreed viewer settings and a screenshot of each render view. Build the render setup to match them.
  • One file, reused: one render setup with 9 cameras and 2 scenes (Studio and Dark Space). Each platform reuses it; only the model changes.
  • How: build it once as APEX_Render_Template and upload it for approval. After approval, start every render file from it. Do not copy cameras into your own scene: the color settings do not come with them.
  • 9 cameras: 01_Hero, then 02 to 09 for the standard views (front, sides, back, top, bottom, detail). Exact angles come from the screenshots.CONFIRM names
  • 2 environments, packed in the file: Studio (clean product lighting) and Dark Space (black background, hard sun light).
  • Cycles. Color Management: ACES 1.3, exposure 1.0. 2048 × 2048, transparent background. No extra lights.
  • Renders run in VNTANA Advanced Renders from the attached .blend.

7Model rules

Geometry

  • Real-world size in meters, from the CAD VNTANA sends you. This CAD is the outside only: VNTANA removes every part hidden from outside before you receive it, separately for each pose. Model only what is in the CAD; do not add internal parts. When a platform has several poses, build parts from the most open pose (see the platform page).
  • Repeatable parts: catalog screws, washers, nuts and connectors appear many times. Model each part number once and place linked copies (instances) everywhere it appears.
  • Combine to keep draw calls down. On the central body nothing moves (no exploded view is planned), so merge small parts by type: brackets with brackets, cables with cables, and so on. On the solar array wings, keep every part that moves in the animation separate. Instance the panels too if the animation still works.
  • Keep the CAD as it is where it looks rough: holes in the CAD stay open (no plates behind them; Apex's own images show them), and wires that run into other parts stay that way. Where two identical meshes sit exactly on top of each other, keep one.
  • Clean geometry where visible.
  • Triangle budget: no hard limit for now. VNTANA removes hidden geometry when the GLB is uploaded. Keep it as light as accuracy allows: AR is the one place a model this large struggles.

Naming

  • Every part or merged group is a named mesh with the platform prefix: NOVA_, ARIES_, COMET_. For example NOVA_BUS, NOVA_SADA_L, NOVA_ARRAY_L1.
  • Each configurator option is a parent empty named OPT_<GROUP>_<CHOICE>, for example OPT_COMMS_MDL.
  • Moving parts (hinges, SADAs, yokes) have their pivot on the real rotation axis.
  • English names, no spaces, no default names like "Cube.001".

Origin, scale, orientation

  • Origin at the center of the launch interface (separation ring) face. +Z up, toward the payload deck.CONFIRM
  • All transforms applied on static parts (scale 1).

UVs and textures

  • UVs only where a texture is needed. First list (VNTANA will add to it): solar cells (MAT_SOLAR_CELL), solar panel backing mesh (MAT_ARRAY_MESH), swirl-milled boxes 2 and 3 (MAT_AL_MILLED), silver and gold MLI blankets, silver-coated Teflon covers (MAT_SCT), Kapton tape, black Kapton. Flat finishes (chromate, gold rail, anodize, stainless, paint, harness) are plain material values and need no UVs (no UVs or a quick auto-unwrap are both fine).
  • Any UV tool is fine (Maya, RizomUV, Zen UV). Automatic layouts are OK as long as the texture reads correctly.
  • Expect UVs on about a third of the model, and textures on 4 or 5 materials. Everything else is a plain material.
  • Use the CAD as is. Remodel a part only if you have to: it does not look right, you cannot get clean UVs on it, or it does not move cleanly in the animation (the solar panel backing plates are the likely case). Tell us before you remodel, and keep the CAD sizes and positions exactly.
  • Single 0 to 1 UV space per material; texture density at real-world scale.
