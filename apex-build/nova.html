Do not model test equipment. The Nova 1 photos were taken on a vibration test table. Leave out the test table, the aluminum test feet the satellite sits on, loose blue and orange tape on the table, red tags, and loose test cables. Model only the satellite.
  • The launch-side deck photo is the real outside. It shows the separation ring, boxes and harness, all in the CAD: model them. Leave out what is only there for handling: silver protective bags, zip-tie tails, red tags.
  • Stowed layout (from the photos): the solar array folds flat against the side of the bus (4 panels in a row are visible), held under gold perforated rails with black spring stacks at the ends. Use the CAD for exact positions.
  • Reference images and CAD: in the Apex workspace on VNTANALINK TBD. Use the photos for materials and surface detail, the CAD for every size and position.

How to use the two CAD files

  • Use only these two assets in the Apex workspace: Nova CAD: Deployed and Nova CAD: Stowed. They are the cleaned-up versions. Ignore any other Nova files you find. You need a VNTANA login with access to the Apex workspace to open them.
  • The deployed file is the master. VNTANA removed internals by deleting every part hidden from outside, separately in each file. So the stowed file is missing parts that only the folded wings hide, such as the solar array drive bodies. Build every part from the deployed file, and use the stowed file only for the stowed positions.
  • The animated asset needs every part from both poses. Nothing may pop in or out while the wings unfold.
  • Line the files up. The stowed file sits about 42 / -106 / -2 mm (X / Y / Z) away from the deployed one. Move both so the separation ring face is the shared origin (section 5).
  • Delete the zero-width lines. The cable assembly on the +Z end plate contains lines 12.345 m long with no width (CAD leftovers). They do not render but make the bounding box 13.8 m tall and break framing.CONFIRM with Apex
  • Turn the model upright. In the CAD the stack axis (separation ring to payload deck) points along the viewer's depth axis, not up. Rotate so +Z points from the separation ring face toward the payload deck.
  • Product images: the renders and line drawings on the Nova page show the overall shape and the two solar array wings.

  • Repeatable parts: model once, place copies. 1,312 catalog screws, washers, nuts and connectors (84 part numbers). Model each part number once and place it as a linked copy (instance) everywhere it appears. A fix to one updates every copy.
  • Detail: Full = large or seen up close, full detail. Normal = visible but smaller. Simple = small or hard to see, keep it light.
  • Do not model the reaction wheels and bulkheads left inside the closed panel box.
  • Names are proposed. Apex is reviewing the part IDs marked CONFIRM. The parts manifest (in the Apex workspace) has the CAD part numbers for every row. If the CAD shows a part this list misses, add it with the same naming and tell us.

5Pre-delivery QA

  1. Real-world size from the VNTANA CAD, in meters: deployed 15.65 × 0.72 × 1.76 m, stowed 1.16 × 0.72 × 2.05 m, payload deck 935 × 1185 mm.
  2. Zero-width CAD lines deleted: the bounding box matches the sizes above (not 13.8 m tall).
  3. Every part named with NOVA_; options grouped under OPT_ empties.
  4. Repeatable parts are linked copies of one source per part number.
  5. Parts hidden by the folded wings (solar array drives) are present in the stowed and animated assets.
  6. Hide each option group: nothing else disappears, nothing floats.
  7. Three assets uploaded: NOVA_Deployed, NOVA_Stowed, NOVA_Animated, each with its own GLB and .blend. Stowed fits the launch envelope in the CAD.
  8. NOVA_Animated: 10-second DEPLOY clip, stowed to deployed, no parts passing through each other; pivots on the real hinge and SADA axes.
  9. Only approved library materials (MAT_ names).
  10. Render files (after the viewer review): built from the approved render template; 9 cameras match the agreed screenshots; check camera 01_Hero in Rendered view in both environments.
  11. All transforms applied; origin at the separation ring, +Z up.
  12. All textures packed; the GLB looks the same in the VNTANA viewer as in Blender.
